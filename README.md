# Ethics and Legality Integration into the Agile Software Development Pipeline

The report proposes a progressive model for integrating ethical values, legal obligations, and responsibility mechanisms into an Agile software development pipeline.

The work starts from a standard Agile/Scrum sprint loop and extends it with:

- Value Sensitive Design and Conceptual Engineering, to make stakeholders, values, and ambiguous concepts explicit during backlog refinement and sprint planning.
- Requirement Engineering with GORE and SLEEC, to translate value-derived norms into structured goals and reviewable rules.
- Compliance-as-Code, to turn selected ethical and legal requirements into executable CI/CD policy checks and audit evidence.
- MLOps, design-time assurance, runtime assurance, and meaningful human control, to address AI-specific risks such as bias, model drift, operational uncertainty, and responsibility gaps.

The central idea is to move ethical and legal review from a late, external activity into a continuous engineering process that connects values, requirements, policies, monitoring, and evidence.
